Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 18, 2026

TaxKeeper is a 100% offline application. TaxKeeper does not collect, transmit, or share any personal user data. TaxKeeper has no internet permission and operates entirely on your device. All your financial data remains exclusively on your device and under your control.

1. Information We Collect

TaxKeeper is designed as a privacy-first, offline application. We collect minimal technical data solely for app stability and crash reporting purposes, as described below.

1.1 Information You Provide

All data you enter into TaxKeeper (invoices, expenses, client information, tax estimates) is stored exclusively on your device. We do not have access to this data. It is not transmitted to our servers, stored in any cloud service, or shared with any third party.

1.2 Information Collected Automatically

TaxKeeper uses Firebase Crashlytics (from Google) to improve app stability. Crashlytics may collect the following anonymous, non-personal technical data when a crash occurs:

  • Device model and operating system version
  • App version and build number
  • Stack trace and crash logs (no personal data)
  • Device identifier (used only for crash grouping, not linked to you)

This data is used only for debugging and improving app stability. It cannot be used to identify you and is not combined with any personally identifiable information.

1.3 No Data Collection for Advertising

TaxKeeper does not collect any data for advertising purposes. We do not use any advertising SDKs or tracking technologies.

1.4 No Analytics SDK

TaxKeeper does not include Google Analytics, Firebase Analytics, or any third-party analytics SDK.

2. Third-Party Services

The following third-party services are used in TaxKeeper. Each operates under its own privacy policy:

Firebase Crashlytics Google LLC Crash logs, device OS, app version Crash reporting & app stability

These services may collect data as governed by their respective privacy policies. We encourage you to review Google's Privacy Policy at https://policies.google.com/privacy.

3. Data Storage and Security

3.1 Local Storage Only

All user data is stored locally on your device using an encrypted SQLite database (SQLCipher). Your data is protected by your device's security credentials (fingerprint, PIN, or password) that you use to unlock your device.

3.2 Data Retention

TaxKeeper retains your data on your device indefinitely until you explicitly delete the app or delete the app's data through your device's settings. We do not have the ability to access, modify, or delete your data remotely.

3.3 Data Deletion by User

You can delete all your data at any time by:

  • Uninstalling TaxKeeper from your device, or
  • Clearing TaxKeeper's data through your device's Settings > Apps > TaxKeeper > Clear Data

4. Your Rights Under GDPR (European Union Users)

If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA), you have the following rights under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR):

Right of Access Request a copy of your personal data Export data from app Settings
Right to Rectification Request correction of inaccurate data Edit data directly in the app
Right to Erasure Request deletion of your data Uninstall app or clear data
Right to Restriction Request restriction of processing Contact developer below

Since TaxKeeper is a 100% offline app with no server-side processing, most GDPR obligations are inherently satisfied. We do not process your data on any server. To exercise your rights, please contact us using the information below.

5. Your Rights Under CCPA (California Residents)

If you are a California resident, you have the following rights under the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA):

5.1 Right to Know

You have the right to know what personal information we collect, use, and share. TaxKeeper collects only anonymous crash reporting data through Firebase Crashlytics. We do not sell your personal information.

5.2 Right to Delete

You have the right to request deletion of your personal information. Since all your data is stored only on your device, you can delete it by uninstalling the app or clearing app data.

5.3 Right to Opt-Out of Sale

TaxKeeper does not sell your personal information. There is nothing to opt out of.

5.4 Non-Discrimination

We will not discriminate against you for exercising your privacy rights.

5.5 Authorized Agent

You may use an authorized agent to exercise your rights on your behalf. Please contact us below with written authorization.

6. Children's Privacy

TaxKeeper is not intended for use by children under the age of 13 (or 16 in certain jurisdictions). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us immediately.

7. International Data Transfers

TaxKeeper is a 100% offline application. We do not transfer your data internationally because your data never leaves your device and is not transmitted to any server.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or legal requirements. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. We encourage you to review this Privacy Policy periodically.
