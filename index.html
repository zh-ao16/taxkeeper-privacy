Freelance Ledger — Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 28, 2026

App: Freelance Ledger (Android), package com.freelanceledger.app

Publisher: Freelance Ledger (individual developer, GitHub: zh-ao16) — zhaohai1696@gmail.com

Freelance Ledger is a 100% offline application. Freelance Ledger does not collect, transmit, or share any personal user data. Freelance Ledger has no internet permission and operates entirely on your device. All your financial data remains exclusively on your device and under your control.

1. About this policy

This policy explains what the Freelance Ledger Android app does with your information. It applies to the Freelance Ledger app published on Google Play. Freelance Ledger is a record-keeping tool for freelancers: it stores invoices, invoice line items, clients, payments and expenses on your device only.

2. The short version

  • There is no account to create and no sign-in.
  • Your records are stored in the app's private storage on your device and are never transmitted to us.
  • We do not collect, sell, rent or share any personal or financial information. There is no advertising, no analytics and no crash-reporting service in the app.
  • You can export everything you entered, and you can delete it, at any time — see §7 and §8.

3. Information the app stores on your device

Freelance Ledger stores the records you create, so that the app can work offline:

  • invoices and invoice line items (client name, client email, amounts, tax rate, currency, notes);
  • client records (name, email, tax rate);
  • payment records (amount, date, method note);
  • expense records (amount, date, category, note, optional linked client);
  • app settings, reminder preferences and a cached record of whether you own the one-time upgrade.

This data is kept in the app's private storage (filesDir + a local database). It stays on your device and is never sent to us or to anyone else. The app contains no advertising identifier, no analytics SDK and no crash-reporting SDK.

4. Information we do not collect

We do not collect any personal or sensitive user data: no name, email address, phone number, physical address, payment or financial information, photos, files, contacts, location, health, or advertising identifiers are collected by us. Because the app declares no internet permission, it has no ability to transmit your records to a server.

5. Permissions the app uses

  • Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS) — used only to show the invoice reminders you configure, on your device.
  • Boot completed (RECEIVE_BOOT_COMPLETED) — used only so the app can restore its scheduled invoice reminder after the device restarts. It carries no personal data.
  • Wake lock / network state — declared by the Android libraries the app uses for scheduled work and for Google Play purchases. The app itself does not open network connections, read your network state for tracking, or run advertising.

6. Purchases and payments

The optional one-time upgrade is sold through Google Play's billing system (Google Play Billing Library). Google Play processes and stores your payment details; the app never receives, stores or transmits your card or bank information. There is no purchase inside the app that uses any other payment method. The app records only whether the upgrade is owned, and it re-checks that with Google Play when you open the upgrade screen.

7. Exporting your data

The app can export the data you entered, only to a destination you choose:

  • CSV export of clients and invoices, saved to the app's export folder and shared through the Android system share sheet;
  • Invoice PDF generation, shared the same way;
  • Encrypted backup file (PBKDF2 + AES-256-GCM, with a passphrase you choose), written to a location you pick with the Android file picker.

The app does not upload these files anywhere. Once you export a file, it is outside the app's control and is handled by the app or service you chose to send it to.

8. Retention and deletion

8.1 No server copies.

Because your data is never transmitted, we hold no copy of it. When you uninstall Freelance Ledger, all of it is removed from your device.

8.2 Deleting data while the app is installed.

Clearing app data deletes the invoices, invoice items, clients and payment records stored in the app. Expense records created in this version are removed when you uninstall the app.

8.3 Back up first.

Uninstalling the app permanently deletes your records. Export a backup first if you want to keep them.

9. Security

Your records are stored in the app's private storage, which Android isolates per app. The local database is not additionally encrypted by the app (it relies on the device's own protections, such as a device screen lock and file-based encryption). Exported backup files are encrypted with AES-256-GCM using a passphrase you set. Data is not transmitted over a network, so there is no in-transit encryption to report.

10. Children's privacy

Freelance Ledger is a business record-keeping tool and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone, of any age.

11. Changes to this policy

If we change how the app handles information, we will update this page and change the "Effective date" above. The version of the app in use with this policy is version 1.0.0.
